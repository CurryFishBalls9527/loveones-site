关系管家 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 12 日（10 月 12 日更新：聊天名字）

关系管家是一个私密的 App。我们没有账号系统，没有服务器，不收集也不分析你的任何数据。

你的数据存在哪里

  • 你添加的人、写下的记录、口袋、心情、给自己的信等，都只保存在你的 iPhone 上。
  • 如果你开启 iCloud 同步（Pro），这些数据会通过 Apple 的 iCloud 同步到你自己登录同一 Apple ID 的设备。我们无法访问你的 iCloud 数据。私密空间的内容也会同步，但在其他设备上默认不显示入口。
  • 你可以在设置里导出全部数据；删除 App 即删除本机数据。

我们会用到的权限

  • 通讯录：只在你从系统通讯录中选择联系人时读取你选中的那几个人的名字、电话和生日。我们不会读取或上传整个通讯录。
  • 麦克风与语音识别：只在你按住说话时使用，在手机本地转成文字，不保存录音。
  • 面容 ID：用于锁住私密空间，由系统完成验证，我们拿不到你的面容数据。
  • 通知：所有提醒都在本机生成。私密空间的提醒默认关闭，开启后也只会写「今晚过得怎么样？」，不包含任何名字。

AI

「上次聊到」使用 Apple 设备上的端侧模型，只读取你自己写的记录，在本机运行，不会发送到任何服务器。它不会分析任何人，也不会替你写消息。

购买

付费通过 Apple 的 App Store 完成。我们收不到你的付款信息。

分享

只有「这一年」白天部分可以生成分享图片，默认不显示名字，也不包含你写的记录。私密空间里的任何内容都不能分享。分享扩展只记录你主动选择分享给关系管家的文字。

当你把聊天记录分享给关系管家时，App 会在你的手机上记下对方在聊天 App 里显示的名字（例如「老妈」），用来下次认出是在和谁聊。只有你在分享时打开「记住」才会保存；这些名字只存在你的手机（和你开启的 iCloud）里，可以在设置 › 分享聊天 里随时清除。被设为「保持距离」的人，这些名字会被删除。

不做的事

  • 没有广告，没有第三方统计或追踪 SDK。
  • 不读取你的通话记录、短信或其他 App 的内容。
  • 不出售、不共享任何数据，因为我们根本没有收集。

儿童

关系管家不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集任何人的个人信息。

联系我们

LoveOnes Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026 (updated Oct 12: chat names)

LoveOnes is a private app. There are no accounts and no servers, and we don’t collect or analyze any of your data.

Where your data lives

  • The people you add, your notes, pockets, moods and letters to yourself are stored only on your iPhone.
  • If you turn on iCloud sync (Pro), Apple’s iCloud syncs this data to your own devices signed in to the same Apple ID. We can’t access your iCloud data. Your private space syncs too, but its entry stays hidden on other devices by default.
  • You can export everything from Settings. Deleting the app deletes the data on your phone.

Permissions we use

  • Contacts: only when you pick people from the system contact picker, and only the name, phone number and birthday of the people you pick. We never read or upload your whole address book.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: only while you hold to talk. Speech is turned into text on your phone and no audio is kept.
  • Face ID: locks your private space. The system does the check; we never see your face data.
  • Notifications: every reminder is created on your phone. Private-space reminders are off by default, and when on they only say “How’s tonight going?” with no names.

AI

“Last time” uses Apple’s on-device model. It reads only the notes you wrote, runs on your phone, and never sends anything to a server. It doesn’t analyze anyone and never writes messages for you.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple’s App Store. We never receive your payment details.

Sharing

Only the daytime part of “Your year” can be shared as an image; names are hidden by default and your notes are never included. Nothing from your private space can be shared. The share extension only records text you choose to share with LoveOnes.

When you share a chat to LoveOnes, the app can remember, on your phone, the name the other person shows up as in that chat app (for example “Mama”), so it recognizes them next time. It’s saved only when you leave “Remember” on while sharing, stays on your phone (and your iCloud, if you turned sync on), and can be cleared any time in Settings › Shared chats. These names are deleted for anyone you move to your private space.

What we don’t do

  • No ads, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We don’t read your call log, text messages, or other apps.
  • We don’t sell or share data, because we don’t collect any.

Children

LoveOnes isn’t directed to children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect anyone’s personal information.
